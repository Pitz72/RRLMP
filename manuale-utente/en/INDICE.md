# Runtime Live Machine Pro — User Manual
## Table of Contents — English Edition (v1.15.10, Second Edition)

---

**Chapter 1 — Runtime Live Machine Pro: a philosophy**
What RLMP is and who it was built for. The “Single Show” philosophy. The Main-Side-Heavy architecture. The broadcast grid: a visual grammar.

**Chapter 2 — Installation and first launch**
System requirements. Installing on Windows, macOS and Linux. The welcome screen. The first launch: what to expect.

**Chapter 3 — The working interface**
The Control Bar: logo, On Air timer, studio clock, Master Volume, VU meter, STOP ALL, File and Tools menus. The six-column grid: headers, DEAD AIR warning, colour customization. The audio card: anatomy, status badges, interaction.

**Chapter 4 — The basic workflow: load and play**
Importing by drag & drop. Supported formats. Playing and stopping. The per-column exclusion logic. Organizing the running order: reordering and multiple selection. Structure cues: Intro and Outro.

**Chapter 5 — Clip properties and the Waveform Editor**
Basic properties: name, colour, Gain. The waveform editor: navigation, zoom, playhead. The four handles. Auto-Trim and Smart Cues. Transition preview. Behaviours (Normal, Stacco). Next Action. Fade In and Fade Out. Assigning controls.

**Chapter 6 — The mixing engine**
The audio hierarchy. Automatic ducking. Music Dominance. Stacchi: the exception to the rule. Volume levelling (loudness). Master Chain: HPF, multiband glue, limiter.

**Chapter 7 — The Pad FX and the Automix view**
The pad FX “jingle machine”: overlapping playback, quick and full configuration, positioning. The Automix view: the Music-column deck, BPM compatibility, beat-matched transitions, automatic mode.

**Chapter 8 — Hardware, keyboard and MIDI**
Audio routing: device selection, USB mixers, latency. Keyboard commands: global shortcuts, per-clip keys. MIDI controllers: connection, MIDI Learn, message types, portability.

**Chapter 9 — Session recording**
Starting the recording. What gets recorded. The internal format. Export: WAV, FLAC, MP3, OGG, WEBM. Quality options. Practical considerations.

**Chapter 10 — Project management and data safety**
The `.lmp` project file. Quick Save and Save As. Protection on close. Auto-Backup and autosave. Export project with audio. Integrity check.

**Chapter 11 — Remote Control**
The local browser server. Activation and PIN. Connecting from a tablet or phone. What you control remotely. Security and limits.

**Chapter 12 — Updates**
The auto-updater. Startup behaviour and manual check. The “never during a live show” rule. Platform differences.

**Chapter 13 — Advanced features**
NoteBoard: the script in the control room. Column colour customization. Transitions: Crossfade, Segue, Gapless. The General Settings window. Playout Log. Undo/Redo. Toast notification system.

**Chapter 14 — Troubleshooting and FAQ**
Audio problems: silence, artefacts, levels. Red clips and missing files. MIDI problems. Startup problems. Frequently asked questions.
