# v1.15.34 — Measured limiter and «What's new» screen

**Type:** audio fix · distribution standard alignment · **Date:** 2026-09-18

## The master limiter really holds its ceiling

The limiter at the end of the master chain was **measured** inside the program, with the same method used on StreamFlow, and it had two faults:

- **it raised the whole programme by half a decibel** (+0.57 dB), even quiet music it was not supposed to touch. The audio component of the Chromium engine adds this gain on its own, in proportion to the threshold;
- **it did not hold the ceiling you set**: with the ceiling at −1 dBFS and very loud signals the output went above 0 dBFS (measured +0.5 dBTP on a real track with the Glue off).

The limiter now works slightly below the chosen ceiling (1.5 dB) and a fixed gain cancels exactly that extra half decibel. Measured result:

- below the threshold the signal passes through **unchanged**, to a hundredth of a decibel;
- peaks stay **below the ceiling** for every slider value, up to 12 dB of overload;
- on a dynamic track the level does not change; on an already heavily compressed master (−9 LUFS, peaks at 0 dBFS) the limiter now actually works and takes off half an LU.

**Multiband Glue:** it had been calibrated with the old limiter in the path, that is with that extra half decibel. It has been recalibrated: **with the Glue on, the level stays where it was** (measured: within 0.1 LU).

The «Maximum threshold» slider in Settings (Master Chain) now shows the **real output ceiling**.

## «What's new» screen after updating

After every update, on first launch a window shows a short summary of what is new and a link to the full release notes. It appears **only once** per version and **never on a fresh install**.
