# v1.15.39 — MIX mode: the end-of-track mix always starts

**Type:** fix (MIX mode, "Auto at end of track"; file duration) · **Date:** 2026-11-06

In the MIX mode test on 6 November, with "Auto at end of track" on, some tracks stopped at the end instead of mixing into the next one. The playout log showed 2 to 14 seconds of silence between the end of one track and the start of the next. There were two causes.

## The remaining time was counted to the wrong point

The MIX view decided when to start the mix by looking at how much was left until the end **of the file**. The player, however, stops earlier, at the **effective** end: the file minus its trimmed tail of silence. The longer the trimmed tail, the later the mix started.

- **"A Cena, Per Esempio"** (4.2 s of trimmed tail), with the short mix: it started half a second before the real end and was cut off.
- The other way round, when the change was a classic crossfade (an uncertain beat grid, very common in progressive rock), the mix started 6.9 s before the end: with a 2 s crossfade, the last 5 seconds of the outgoing track were lost.

Now the time is counted to the effective end. The lead depends on the kind of change:
- **beat-matched mix**: the chosen length in beats (short, medium, long) plus 2.5 s to lock on, as before;
- **classic crossfade**: the crossfade length plus 1 s. The outgoing track plays almost to its end.

## Some files declare a false duration

Ten FLAC files out of 49 by Banco del Mutuo Soccorso carry a wrong duration in their header. The program, and the internal audio player too, took it at face value:

| Track | Declared duration | Real duration |
|---|---|---|
| Baciami Alfredo | 5:21 | 1:42 |
| Buonanotte, sogni d'oro | 5:12 | 2:56 |
| Metamorfosi | 10:54 | 3:41 |
| Ciclo | 4:22 | 1:35 |

On these tracks, at the real end, the program still thought there were minutes left. The automatic mix never started. The 2 s fade-out at the end never started either, so the track stopped abruptly. The duration and the remaining time on the card were wrong as well.

Now the silence analysis, which already decodes the whole file, **measures the real duration** and saves it in the clip. The player uses it for the end of the track, the fade-out, the trim and the mix. In projects already saved, MUSIC and PRE-SHOW clips are analysed once more to measure the duration: trims are left untouched. If a duration was wrong a notice appears, and the project is marked as changed so the fix can be saved.

In the folders of the Festival della Canzone Artificiale first semi-final and of Sanremo 2026 no file has this problem.

## Technical details

- `automixEngine.ts`: `autoTransitionLeadSec` (lead for beat-matched mix or classic crossfade) and `remainingToEffectiveEndSec`; `AutomixView` uses them instead of the raw-duration calculation.
- `StreamPlayer`: with `clip.durationDecoded` the measured duration replaces the audio element's in `getDuration()` and in the end-of-track checks (fade-out, trim, PreEnd). The metadata duration never replaces it.
- `utils/silenceDetection.ts`: `decideSilenceUpdate` writes `duration` and `durationDecoded`; `durationOnly` case for clips already checked by 1.15.38; `needsSilenceAnalysis` for the load-time pass. `loadClip` no longer overwrites a measured duration.
- Tests: 9 new on the end-of-track mix and on the player (the fade-out starts at the real end even with a wrong header), 4 on the measured duration. Vitest 392/392, typecheck 0 errors on all 4 processes.
