# v1.15.38 — Automatic silence trim rewritten

**Type:** serious fix (automatic silence trim, Smart Cues) · **Date:** 2026-11-05

The automatic silence trim could start a track halfway through, or cut off its ending. It affected the MUSIC and PRE-SHOW columns: when tracks were added, when a project was loaded, and with the Auto-Trim button. It had been like this since 1.2.15.

## What was wrong

The detector looked for silences across the whole track. It then took "the first silence ending in the first half" as the leading silence, and "the last one starting in the second half" as the trailing silence. It never checked that the silence actually touched the start or the end of the file. A quarter-second pause inside the song was enough to move the start or the end of the track there.

Measured on 382 real tracks (the first semi-final of the Festival della Canzone Artificiale 2026, Sanremo 2026, Genesis, Banco, PFM, Alan Parsons, jazz):

- **Orfèo Nebulante, "Splash! Boing! Zac-zac-zà!"** (4:03): a 0.28 s pause at 1:45, so the track started at 1:45;
- **Dj Dade, "Le tengo io"**: the last 87 seconds removed; **Fabio, "Volpe"**: the last 25.7 removed;
- **Francesco Renga, "Il meglio di me"**: started at 1:12; **Alan Parsons, "Where's the Walrus"**: the last 92 seconds removed;
- **Genesis, "Dancing With The Moonlit Knight"**: the first 4.6 seconds of a cappella vocals cut;
- **nicola vassallo, "Nero"**: the first hit cut (0.48 s); **Enrico, "Cydia"**: 0.77 s of the opening fade-in.

Beyond the serious cases, the old detector cut more than 0.3 s of real sound on 36 openings and 163 endings out of 382: fade-outs that could still be heard, and soft attacks. On very loud masters its threshold reached −35 dB.

## How it works now

- **Only the edges are trimmed.** Only what comes before the first sound and after the last one is removed. Pauses inside the track no longer count, however long.
- **Threshold tied to the track.** Anything within 45 dB of the loud parts of the track counts as sound, and so does any clear peak within 35 dB: whispers and isolated hits stay.
- **End-of-file clicks.** Many generated tracks end with a tiny ramp of noise in the last 40 ms. It no longer blocks trimming the tail.
- **Trailing hiss.** If a track ends in steady background hiss, the hiss is removed, but never above 40 dB below the loud parts. At the start the threshold never rises: a soft opening pad is music.
- **Measured guarantee.** On all 382 tracks the new detector never removes more than the old one did (by over 0.3 s), at either end.

## Projects already saved

The first time a project is opened, every MUSIC and PRE-SHOW clip is analysed again, once:
- if the saved trim is exactly what the old detector produces on the same file, it wrote that trim, and the trim is **recalculated**;
- if it differs, you changed it, and it **stays as it is**.

A notice says how many clips had their trim recalculated. The project is marked as changed: save it to keep the fix. On the semi-final project "Prima Serata": 57 clips recalculated and 4 left as they were. Those 4 (Orfèo, "Volpe", "Le tengo io", "Nero") are exactly the ones where the old detector got it wrong and the trim had already been fixed by hand.

## Other fixes

- **Auto-Trim** in the clip window: when it finds no silence at the edges it now resets the fields to zero. Before, it kept the previous value, even a wrong one.
- **Smart Cues** (suggested Intro/Outro markers): a quiet stretch counts as an intro only if it starts at the beginning of the file, and as an outro only if it reaches the end. Before, the outro could land on a pause inside the track, and in PRE-SHOW the outro marker starts the next track.
- **Phone page**: long pad names no longer break in the middle of a word.

## Technical details

- New `src/main/silenceTrim.ts` (pure logic): energy in 10 ms blocks on 24 kHz stereo PCM, thresholds and rules above; parsing of `silencedetect` regions and choice of Smart Cues.
- `AudioProcessor.detectSilence` decodes the whole file as a stream (constant memory) and, on request (`withLegacy`), also computes the old detector's fingerprint, identical in its numbers.
- `utils/silenceDetection.ts`: `decideSilenceUpdate` chooses between new trim, recalculation and keeping; new `silenceCheckedV3` gate on clips.
- Tests: 18 new on the trim logic and Smart Cues, 9 on the decision. Vitest 379/379, typecheck 0 errors on all 4 processes.
