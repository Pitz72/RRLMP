# v1.15.37 — FX pads from your phone and MIX mode

**Type:** features (Remote Control, MIX mode) and a fix to beat detection · **Date:** 2026-11-05

Two new things for live shows:
- **FX pads from your phone.** Remote Control was already there, but from a phone you could only use STOP ALL and the Music column. The effect pads (applause, laughter, gong…) were missing. Now the page that opens on a phone, a tablet or another PC is the studio's cartwall: same pads, same colours, and a tap goes on air straight away.
- **MIX mode, by hand too.** With the MIX view open, changing track is always a beat-matched mix, never a hard cut, even when you make the change yourself: click, key, MIDI or phone. It is built for a quick chart countdown (just the start of each track, fast changes) and for a medley while waiting for the televote to close.

## MIX mode

- **Every manual change becomes a mix.** With the MIX view open, a Music track launched while another one is on air no longer cuts it: the change is a mix, as with the TRANSITION button. This covers clicking the track, keys, MIDI notes and PLAY from the phone. With the view closed, nothing changes.
- **Clickable playlist rows.** In the MIX view, clicking any track mixes into it; if nothing is on air, it starts it. Keyboard works too (Enter, Space).
- **Mix length: SHORT (4 beats), MEDIUM (8), LONG (16).** Short is for fast changes. The choice is remembered.
- **NEXT skips missing files** instead of stopping.
- **Outcome of the last change** shown under the buttons: beat-matched mix, classic crossfade (with the reason) or not carried out.
- **From the phone**, MUSIC tab: MIX ON/OFF switch, a large ▶▶ NEXT button with the next track's title and the kind of change ("beat-matched mix" or "classic crossfade"), and the outgoing track marked GOING OUT during the mix. With MIX on, the tracks' PLAY becomes "MIX ▶". Turning MIX on from the phone also opens the MIX view in the studio.

## Beat-matched mix: beats really lined up

Measuring with tracks whose beat is known exactly, and on 57 tracks from Sanremo 2026, turned up three faults that often left the "beat-matched mix" out of phase (full report: `docs/automix/VALIDAZIONE-GRIGLIA-2026-11-05.md`).

- **Fast changes out of phase.** After a mix the incoming track plays slightly faster or slower and returns to normal speed over 20-30 seconds. If you moved to the next track before then, the tempo was worked out from the nominal one: beats 42 ms apart after 2 beats and 139 ms apart by the end of the mix. Now the tempo you actually hear is used, and the offset is zero.
- **Wrong first beat.** The detector placed the first beat 35-90 ms off, and every BPM was 0.23% too high because of a rounding error. On real music, the grid from the first minute missed the beats of the next minute by 141 ms (median). Now tempo and beat are found together, on the kick drum: exact tempos, first beat within 6 ms, and a 3 ms median offset on the tracks that can be locked.
- **Tracks with no beat taken as rhythmic.** A held chord came out "at 147 BPM". Now every track gets a beat-reliability score: below the threshold (ballads, irregular kick) the change is a classic crossfade, never beats locked at random.
- Tracks already analysed are analysed again automatically, once, the first time the project is opened. Until that finishes, the change is a crossfade.
- Choosing a track that is still fading out from the previous change is no longer silently ignored: it leaves at once and comes back in with the mix.
- With one fast change after another, the oldest track leaves within a quarter of a second: never three tracks on top of each other.

## Top bar reorganised

Up to 1.15.36 nearly every control was squeezed to the right, with a large empty space in the middle. The bar now has three zones:
- **left**, project and archive: FILE, Tools, LOG (the list of tracks that went on air, previously an icon among the live controls) and REC;
- **centre**, exactly in the middle, the audio console: ARM, VU, MASTER VOL;
- **right**, live controls and time: FX, MIX, STOP ALL, ON AIR, clock.

From 1800 px wide upwards (Full HD screens) the space gained goes to readability: VU with larger LEDs, a longer and therefore more precise master fader, larger ON AIR and clock, taller buttons, a LOG label. Below 1800 px the bar stays compact and fits in 1366 px even with the microphone armed and a recording waiting to be exported (measured).

## FX pads on the phone

- The **FX** tab comes first: every pad from the FX pad, laid out to fill the screen with no scrolling. If there are many, you move between pages with the ◀ ▶ buttons.
- A pad fires **as soon as your finger touches it**, not when you lift it, like a hardware pad. Tapping it again while it plays stops it, as in the studio. Several effects can play at once.
- A pad on air lights up in its colour with ON AIR, and goes dark by itself when the effect ends.
- Every tap gets a confirmation from the studio. If none arrives within a second and a half, the pad turns red with NOT RECEIVED and the phone vibrates.
- **STOP FX** stops only the effects; the music stays on air. **STOP ALL** stops everything. These two buttons fire on release, so a thumb resting on the edge of the screen cannot stop the show by mistake.
- The **MUSIC** tab lists the Music column with PLAY/STOP, as before.
- The top bar always shows the connection status and the delay in milliseconds.
- If the Wi-Fi drops or the phone goes to sleep, the page notices and reconnects by itself. A tap made while disconnected is not queued: applause that starts ten seconds late is worse than applause that never starts.
- The PIN is remembered on the phone: from the second time on, the page opens already connected.
- It also works with TalkBack and VoiceOver: a double tap fires the pad and its state (on air, file missing) is read out.

## In the studio (Settings → General → Remote Control)

- **QR code**: scan it with the phone camera and the page opens already connected, with no address or PIN to type. The PIN travels in the part of the link after the `#`, which the browser never sends over the network.
- **The PIN no longer changes at every launch.** It changes only with the **New PIN** button; connected devices are disconnected and must enter the new one.
- **Turn on Remote Control when the app opens**: optional, off by default. With the box unticked the server stays off until you turn it on by hand, as before.
- **Connected devices**: the count updates live. You can see at once whether the phone is linked, without trying a pad on air.
- The link to copy and the QR code use the right address. Before, the app took the first address it found, which on a PC with WSL, Hyper-V or VirtualBox could belong to a virtual adapter, and the phone could not find the studio. Now Wi-Fi and wired networks come first, virtual adapters go last and 169.254 addresses (an adapter with no network) are left out.

## Fixes

- After turning Remote Control off and on again, the tablet showed an empty list until something changed in the studio. The state is now kept.
- The buttons in the Remote Control section had no button styling and looked like plain text.
- Automated tests are no longer compiled into the installed program.
- With the phone page already open, scanning a new QR code (after "New PIN") did not read the PIN, because the browser does not reload the page. The page now notices and reconnects.

## Measured timings

On the studio PC, with the real app and the page opened at the Wi-Fi address: 2.6 ms from tap to confirmation, 16 ms from tap to the effect on air. From a phone, add the Wi-Fi time, usually a few milliseconds.

MIX mode, same setup with tracks at 124, 120 and 118 BPM plus one with no beat: START, then NEXT every 2 seconds. Two beat-matched mixes in a row, the second one started while the incoming track was still sped up by the first, then a classic crossfade into the track with no beat.

## Security

Everything stays as before: local network only, a 6-digit PIN from the cryptographic generator, at most 10 attempts every 5 minutes per address, connections from other websites' pages refused. Added: a closed list of accepted commands, every pad commanded must exist in the studio, messages over 4 KB refused, pad colours accepted only as #rrggbb.

## If the phone cannot find the studio

1. Phone and PC on the same Wi-Fi network, not the guest network: it often isolates devices from each other.
2. The program allowed in the Windows firewall. Windows asks the first time: allow it on public networks too if your home network is set as "Public".
3. If the first address does not respond, Settings lists the PC's other addresses.
