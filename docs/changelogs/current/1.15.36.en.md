# v1.15.36 — New "Backlit Pads" interface

**Type:** interface (audio behaviour unchanged) · **Date:** 2026-10-28

The desk has been redesigned from scratch. Of the three directions proposed (Control Room, Signage, Backlit Pads) the third was chosen. Only the look changes: launching, keys, MIDI, transitions, recording and automix work as before.

The board becomes a hardware surface, with raised keys, recessed wells and LEDs in each column's colour. At rest it stays quiet; whatever is on air lights up.

## Board and clips

- The clip on air lights up entirely in its column's colour, with an ON AIR label and a progress bar along the bottom. The text is dark on light colours and white on dark ones, chosen by contrast, so a midnight-blue column reads as well as a yellow one.
- The next track in the queue gets a green ring and LED.
- The clip title has its own line and the artist goes below it. Before, title, artist and keys shared one line and the title was cut to "Midnight…".
- The MUSIC/ASSET label repeated on every card is gone: the column already says it.
- Column headers use two lines: the full title on top, then type, clip count and the column's buttons. "VOCI / PREREGISTRAZIONI" no longer wraps. When the column is about to run out the header turns amber and its LED blinks.
- Missing file: a red MISSING FILE badge and a dark red card. The veil that covered the title is gone.

## ON AIR display

- The fake waveform is replaced by an LED bar that follows the real progress, with elapsed time and duration. LEDs before the end of the intro are cyan, those from the outro marker on are amber, so you can see in advance where the outro falls.
- The ON AIR lamp is lit while a track is on air and dark at rest. The height stays fixed, so the board below never moves.
- UP NEXT also shows artist and duration.

## Top bar

- On the left, the commands you don't need on air (FILE, Tools). On the right, the live ones: microphone, VU, master, FX, MIX, STOP ALL, recording, time on air, clock.
- LED VU meter: 16 LEDs per channel, 3 dB each, with a peak LED that stays lit for a moment.
- FX and MIX have an LED that lights up when the pad or the view is open.
- It fits in 1366 px even with the microphone armed and a recording running.

## Readability

- Larger text: clip titles at 15.5 px (18 px on screens 1920 wide and up), no label below 11 px.
- Contrast measured with the WCAG formula on 24 screens (board on air and idle, FX pad, menus, every settings and clip tab, key map, log, rotation, confirmations, export, MIDI simulator, automix): no text below 4.5:1, or below 3:1 for large text. Disabled buttons are the only exception.
- Switched-off sections in the settings, such as Smart Mic when disabled, were 40% transparent and hard to read. They now turn grey and the text stays clear.
- Blinking text never drops below 60% brightness.
- PRE-SHOW clips that have already played were at 42% opacity. The key now goes flat, with its LED off and light grey text: still distinct, and readable.
- The OFFLINE label on the welcome screen was dark red on a dark background (1.6:1); it is now light red. The same goes for the Info & Updates window.

## Fonts bundled with the app

Manrope for text and Martian Mono for numbers, bundled with the package (OFL licence, about 160 KB). The previous theme loaded Poppins from Google Fonts, but the installed app's security policy blocks external fonts, so the desk actually showed Segoe UI. The fonts now work offline too.

## FX pad, dialogs, automix

The same language applies everywhere. The FX pad is a small 25-key surface and the effect on air lights up in its colour. Dialogs share the same header, close button and buttons. In automix the decks are displays.

With the interface in English, the automix TRANSIZIONE button and the NOTE REGIA header of the notes panel were still in Italian: they now read TRANSITION and BROADCAST NOTES.

## Good to know

- 1.15.35 was never released: updating from 1.15.34 also brings its fixes.
- The user manual, in Italian and English, is updated to the new interface: new screenshots and rewritten descriptions (top bar, ON AIR display, column headers, cards).

## Technical notes

- Theme in `src/renderer/src/styles/pad.css`, under `.theme-pad`; it replaces `spectrum.css`. Fonts from `@fontsource-variable/manrope` and `@fontsource-variable/martian-mono`.
- `utils/colorInk.ts` picks the text colour on lit pads (WCAG luminance), with 4 tests: Vitest 269/269.
- New translation keys: `card.onAirBadge`, `nowPlaying.remaining`.
- The selected clip uses the `picked` class: `sel` is the dialogs' text field.
- Zustand selectors unchanged: no component is subscribed to the whole `activeClips` map again.
